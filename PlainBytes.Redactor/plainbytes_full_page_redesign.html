Why processing stays local

Most redaction tools — especially the AI-powered ones — upload your documents to a server before they can find and remove sensitive information. For anyone bound by confidentiality, privilege, or audit independence rules, that's a risk you shouldn't have to take just to redact a file.

PlainBytes Redactor does all detection and processing on your own device. Your files never leave your computer, and there's no account, no sync, and no server in the loop.
