Privacy Policy — PlainBytes

Last updated: 2025/11/01

This Privacy Policy explains how PlainBytes Studio ("PlainBytes," "we," "us") handles information in connection with our software products, including PlainBytes.MetadataScrubber (collectively, the "Software").

1. Summary

PlainBytes builds offline desktop utilities. The Software does not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data, files, or file contents. All processing takes place entirely on your device. We do not operate any backend server that receives your private data, and the Software does not require an internet connection for its core functions.

2. What the Software does with your files

  • All file processing (including reading, analyzing, and modifying document metadata) happens locally on your device.
  • Your files, or any content or metadata extracted from them, are never uploaded, transmitted, or sent to PlainBytes or any third party.
  • We have no server infrastructure that receives file data, and therefore no ability to access, view, or store your files under any circumstance.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers.
  • File contents or sensitive metadata extracted from your files.
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or precise location data.
  • Any information through cookies, tracking pixels, or similar intrusive technologies.

We do not operate accounts, logins, or user profiles. There is nothing to register, and nothing tied to your identity within the Software.

4. Minimal Anonymous Usage Data

To improve software stability and help us prioritize which features to build next (as part of our multi-tool matrix), we may collect minimal, non-identifiable usage statistics.

  • What is collected: Aggregated counts of feature triggers (e.g., "how many times the Clean button was clicked") and success/error status of tasks.
  • Privacy Guarantee: This data is purely functional, contains zero personal identifiers, zero filenames, and zero file content. It is processed in aggregate and cannot be linked back to an individual user.
  • Control: You can opt-out of this anonymous telemetry at any time in the Software's settings.

5. Information collected by platforms we distribute through

The Software is distributed through the Microsoft Store. As the platform operator, Microsoft independently collects certain data governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. This may include:

  • Download, installation, and update counts.
  • Aggregated, anonymized reliability diagnostics (crash reports).
  • Payment Processing: If you purchase an optional "Support" add-on, the transaction is handled entirely by Microsoft's commerce systems. PlainBytes never receives, processes, or stores your credit card details, bank info, or billing address.

6. In-app rating prompts

The Software uses the Microsoft Store's native API for ratings. PlainBytes only receives a basic completion status (whether the dialog was shown) and never receives the rating score or review text directly; these are managed by Microsoft.

7. External Links

The Software may contain links to our official website (plainbytesstudio.github.io) or third-party support/sponsorship platforms. Once you click these links and leave the Software, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA Compliance)

As PlainBytes does not collect or store personal data, we do not have a data repository to provide, correct, or delete upon request. Your data remains under your exclusive control on your local device.

9. Data security

Data security for your files is a function of your own device's security. Because no personal data is transmitted to or stored by us, there is no PlainBytes-controlled database that could be compromised in a data breach.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect new products or features. The "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
